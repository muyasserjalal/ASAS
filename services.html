Services

What we design

Custom homes and major renovations, commercial projects and civic work, in South Florida and Jeddah.

How a project runs

  1. 1

    Schematic Design

    We confirm your goals, room list and budget. We study the site, the zoning, the trees, the sun and the wind, then prepare two or three design options and refine the one you choose.

  2. 2

    Design Development

    We develop the approved design into a coordinated building: room sizes, structural and mechanical systems with the engineers, and exterior materials, windows and doors.

  3. 3

    Construction Documents

    We prepare the complete drawings and specifications a contractor needs to price and build, coordinate them with the engineers, and submit them for permit.

  4. 4

    Construction Administration

    We represent you during construction: regular site visits, review of shop drawings and submittals, answers to the contractor's questions, the punch list and the final walkthrough.

Additional services

Bidding and contractor selection

We send the drawings to qualified general contractors, compare the bids and help you choose.

Interior design

Finishes, cabinetry, lighting, fixtures and the furniture layout.

Landscape design

Planting, paving, pool deck and site lighting, designed with a licensed landscape architect.

Why ASAS

No hand-offs. You work with the founding architect on every decision.

Every material and spatial decision is justified by how you will actually live in the space, not by trend.

Cross-cultural fluency

Big-firm rigor, small-firm intimacy
