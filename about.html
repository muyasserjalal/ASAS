The nameأساس

ASAS means foundation.

ASAS — أساس, the Arabic word for foundation — is the starting point of every building and the starting point of this practice. A foundation is unseen once construction is complete, but it is the element every later decision depends on. That is the role ASAS plays for its clients: the quiet, structural basis for work that outlasts trend and ownership.

The name is also personal: a way of carrying an Arabic word, and a discipline of starting from first principles, into a practice built between Miami and Jeddah.

Mission

“We believe architecture is only as strong as its foundation. ASAS designs from first principles — honest structure, real place, and the people who will live with the result.”

The practice

ASAS is an experience-based practice.

It runs on the principal's design judgment and reputation, not on volume or a narrow specialty. It stays deliberately small, and brings on associates only as reputation and referrals justify it.
